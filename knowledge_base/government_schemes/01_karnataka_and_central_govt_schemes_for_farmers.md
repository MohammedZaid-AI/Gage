# Government Schemes for Farmers: Karnataka Department of Agriculture and Central Schemes

## Summary
The Karnataka Department of Agriculture (KSDA) implements a wide portfolio of central and state-sponsored schemes covering seed subsidy, irrigation efficiency, soil health, organic farming, farm mechanization, insurance, and farmer welfare compensation. This document consolidates the schemes most relevant to a sugarcane farmer, sourced directly from official district-level Department of Agriculture pages under the Government of Karnataka.

## Main Information

### National Food Security Mission (NFSM)
Covers Rice, Pulses, and Commercial Crops including **Cotton and Sugarcane**, plus Coarse Cereals/Nutri-Cereals sub-missions. Provides for 100-hectare area cluster demonstrations of improved production technologies and cropping systems, distribution of improved-variety seeds, inputs for Integrated Nutrient and Pest Management, agricultural machinery, pump sets, and drip irrigation sets under subsidy, along with farmer training. Incentives are directly transferred to farmers' bank accounts (DBT) to promote adoption of improved technologies.

### Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) — Micro Irrigation
A centrally sponsored scheme facilitating installation of micro-irrigation units (sprinkler and drip) for all categories of farmers, especially small and marginal farmers, at **90% subsidy**, to increase water-use efficiency.

### Soil Health Card Scheme
Provides periodic soil testing and nutrient management recommendations; soil health cards are issued every 3 years per landholding.

### Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)
A sub-component of the Soil Health Management scheme under the National Mission of Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA); promotes long-term soil fertility building, resource conservation, and chemical-free farming through a cluster approach, with support for input production, quality assurance, value addition, and direct marketing.

### Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) — Karnataka Raitha Suraksha
Implemented in Karnataka from 2016-17 as "Karnataka Raitha Suraksha PMFBY." Full premium structure, coverage details, and current-season figures are documented in the dedicated Insurance document to avoid duplication.

### Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY)
Provides funds for certified/HYV seed supply incentives, agriculture mechanization, soil health enhancement, watershed activities, market infrastructure strengthening, extension-service infrastructure, organic/biofertilizer support, SAU research activities, agro-processing, laboratory strengthening, and animal vaccine production, among other uses.

### Krishi Bhagya
Aimed at improving rainfed agriculture through efficient rainwater management (farm ponds, polythene/alternate lining, diesel pump sets, micro-irrigation, shade nets around farm ponds) and enhancing farm productivity sustainably.

### Compensation Schemes
- Compensation to families of farmers who die by suicide: **₹5.00 lakh per family.**
- Compensation to families of farmers/agricultural labourers who die in agriculture-related accidents (e.g., falling from coconut/areca trees, snakebite): **₹2.00 lakh.**
- Subsidy of **₹20,000** for loss of crop/hay-stacks due to fire.

### Krishi Prashasthi
Productivity awards presented to farmers who achieve higher yields in selected crops, at state, district, and taluk levels.

### Supply of Seeds
Hybrids, high-yielding varieties, and certified/truthfully-labelled seeds of major crops are distributed at subsidized rates: **50% subsidy for general-category farmers, 75% for SC/ST farmers.**

### Farm Mechanization & Agro Processing
Farm machinery distributed at **50% subsidy** to general-category farmers and **90% subsidy** to SC/ST-category farmers; for mini-tractors, up to 90% or a maximum of ₹2.00 lakh subsidy for SC/ST farmers. Agro-processing units and tarpaulins: 90% subsidy for SC/ST farmers, 50% for general farmers.

### Krishi Yanthradhare (Custom Hire and Service Centres)
Establishment of farm-machinery Custom Hire and Service Centres at the hobli (sub-taluk) level, through registered charitable trusts, organizations, and NGOs, to help farmers access agricultural equipment for timely operations without individual ownership cost.

### Krishi Navodyama (Agri Start-ups)
Provides initial financial assistance for establishing agricultural enterprises using the latest innovations/technologies, aiming to improve farmers' access to technology and markets.

### Organic Farming Programs
Includes 100-hectare organic-village-site blocks in every taluk, Karnataka State Organic Certification Agency (KSOCA) certification support, and 14 Regional Federations (covering ~1,300 hectares) for collection, grading, value addition, processing, packing, branding, and marketing of organic produce.

### PM-KISAN
Central scheme providing income support of ₹6,000 per year (in three instalments of ₹2,000) to all eligible farmer families, with 100% central funding.

## Important Recommendations
- Sugarcane farmers in Karnataka should specifically register under NFSM's Commercial Crops component (which explicitly includes sugarcane) to access cluster demonstration benefits, subsidized inputs, and DBT incentives.
- For irrigation efficiency (directly relevant given Karnataka's high per-season irrigation requirement for sugarcane — see Irrigation document), farmers should apply for PMKSY micro-irrigation subsidy (90%) to offset the capital cost of converting from furrow to drip/sprinkler irrigation.
- Farmers should register on Karnataka's **FRUITS portal** (Farmers Registration and Unified Beneficiary Information System) to obtain a Farmer ID (FID), which is required to access benefits across Agriculture, Horticulture, and allied departments, including PM-KISAN, crop insurance, and crop-loss compensation.

## Key Facts
- PMKSY micro-irrigation subsidy: 90% for all farmer categories.
- Farm mechanization subsidy: 50% (general) / 90% (SC/ST), with up to ₹2.00 lakh for SC/ST mini-tractor purchase.
- Farmer suicide compensation: ₹5.00 lakh per family; agriculture-accident death compensation: ₹2.00 lakh.
- PM-KISAN: ₹6,000/year per eligible farmer family, paid in three instalments.
