# Viksit Krishi Sankalp Abhiyan (VKSA-2025): Current National Farmer-Scientist Outreach Campaign

## Summary
Viksit Krishi Sankalp Abhiyan (VKSA-2025) is a current, active ICAR/Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare nationwide campaign (29 May – 12 June 2025) that deployed roughly 2,000 outreach teams across 2,170 locations in 700+ districts, including participation from 731 Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), to connect over 1.5 crore farmers directly with agricultural scientists. Karnataka was an explicit part of this campaign, with the state launch held at ICAR-IIHR (Indian Institute of Horticultural Research), Bengaluru.

## Main Information
- VKSA-2025 was launched under the guidance of Union Agriculture Minister Shri Shivraj Singh Chouhan, framed as part of the "Viksit Bharat 2047" vision announced by the Prime Minister.
- **Scale**: approximately 2,000 outreach teams operated in 2,170 locations across more than 700 districts nationwide, with participation from 731 KVKs.
- **Objective**: to close the "lab-to-land" gap by fostering direct interaction between farmers and agricultural experts/scientists, disseminating information on soil health, and collecting ground-level farmer feedback to inform future research and policy.
- **Structure**: the campaign is designed to run twice a year, per the official VKSA guideline, focusing on farmer-scientist interactions, dissemination of soil health information, and structured feedback collection on agricultural innovations, with formal team formation, meeting arrangements, and budget allocation for outreach.
- **Karnataka-specific note**: the campaign was launched in Karnataka at ICAR-IIHR, Bengaluru, on 8 June 2025, where the Union Agriculture Minister specifically praised Karnataka's farming practices, describing them as "the best in the country" and noting the well-thought-out and modern techniques used by farmers in the state.
- Individual ICAR institutes ran their own VKSA-2025 field programs; for example, ICAR-Indian Institute of Oilseeds Research (IIOR), Hyderabad formed scientist teams that visited approximately 450 villages and reached around 60,000 farmers during the campaign window specifically for oilseed crop awareness ahead of the Kharif season — illustrating the institute-specific, crop-focused nature of individual VKSA outreach efforts (a similar sugarcane-focused outreach effort from ICAR-IISR was not independently confirmed in this search pass and should be checked directly with IISR/AICRP-Sugarcane for VKSA-2025-specific sugarcane village-level activity).

## Important Recommendations
- Given VKSA operates through the existing KVK network (731 KVKs nationwide) and is designed to run twice yearly, any Karnataka sugarcane farmer advisory tool should treat VKSA outreach windows as a channel for surfacing current, hyper-local advisory content (soil health, crop-specific guidance) rather than a static knowledge source — the campaign itself generates farmer feedback and localized recommendations that are not centrally published as a single document.
- Because VKSA explicitly emphasizes soil health dissemination, this campaign should be cross-referenced with the Soil Health Card scheme (see the Soil Health document) as a complementary current outreach mechanism, not a standalone data source.
- For a genuinely current, sugarcane-specific KVK advisory, the recommended next step is to directly contact the relevant Karnataka KVK (e.g., KVK Mandya, given the AICRP-Sugarcane centre is located at V.C. Farm, Mandya) for their most recent VKSA-linked or season-specific sugarcane advisory material, since this was not retrievable as a standalone published document in this research pass.

## Key Facts
- Campaign window: 29 May – 12 June 2025 (first 2025 edition; VKSA is designed to run twice per year).
- Scale: ~2,000 teams, 2,170 locations, 700+ districts, 731 KVKs, targeting over 1.5 crore farmers nationally.
- Karnataka state launch: 8 June 2025, at ICAR-IIHR, Bengaluru.
