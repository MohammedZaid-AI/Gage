# Karnataka Farm Mechanization Subsidy and Custom Hire Centres

## Summary
The Karnataka Department of Agriculture operates dedicated subsidy programs to help farmers — particularly small, marginal, and SC/ST farmers — access farm machinery either through direct subsidized purchase or through shared custom-hire infrastructure, reducing the capital barrier to mechanization documented in the IISR technology set.

## Main Information
- **Farm Mechanization & Agro Processing Scheme**: farm machinery is distributed under subsidy to enable farmers to carry out agricultural activities on time and reduce dependence on agricultural labour. Subsidy rates: **50% for general-category farmers, 90% for SC/ST-category farmers.** For mini-tractors specifically, SC/ST farmers can receive up to 90% subsidy or a maximum of ₹2.00 lakh, whichever applies. Agro-processing units and tarpaulins are distributed at 90% subsidy for SC/ST farmers and 50% for general farmers.
- **Krishi Yanthradhare (Custom Hire and Service Centres)**: rather than requiring individual farmers to purchase expensive machinery outright, the department establishes Custom Hire and Service Centres at the hobli (sub-taluk administrative) level, operated through registered charitable trusts, organizations, and societies/NGOs. These centres make agricultural equipment available to farmers for timely operations on a rental/service basis, addressing the affordability gap for machines that individual small farmers cannot justify purchasing.
- This mechanization support is delivered alongside the broader National Food Security Mission (NFSM) framework (which explicitly covers sugarcane as a commercial crop), under which agricultural machinery, pump sets, and drip/sprinkler irrigation sets are also provided under subsidy as part of cluster demonstration programs.

## Important Recommendations
- Small and marginal sugarcane farmers who cannot justify the capital cost of dedicated cutter-planters or ratoon management devices (see the IISR machinery document) should be directed toward their nearest **Krishi Yanthradhare Custom Hire Centre** rather than individual purchase, since the custom-hire model is specifically designed to make such mechanization accessible without full ownership cost.
- SC/ST farmers should be made aware of the substantially higher subsidy tier (90% vs. 50%) available to them under the Farm Mechanization scheme, as this materially changes the economics of mechanized planting/ratoon management adoption.
- Because mechanization subsidy access typically requires registration through Karnataka's FRUITS portal (Farmer ID/FID) and coordination with the local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), farmers should be advised to complete this registration as a prerequisite step before applying for machinery subsidy.

## Key Facts
- General-category farm machinery subsidy: 50%; SC/ST-category: 90% (up to ₹2.00 lakh for mini-tractors).
- Custom Hire Centres are established at the hobli level — the sub-taluk administrative unit — for localized accessibility.
