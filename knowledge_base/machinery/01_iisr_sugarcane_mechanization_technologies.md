# Sugarcane Farm Machinery and Mechanization Technologies (ICAR-IISR)

## Summary
ICAR-IISR, Lucknow has developed a suite of purpose-built sugarcane machinery covering planting, ratoon management, residue handling, and sett-borne disease treatment. These machines are documented as delivering 60–70% cost savings on the specific operations they mechanize, directly relevant to reducing labour dependency and per-hectare cultivation cost.

## Main Information
1. **Ridger-type sugarcane cutter-planter** (PTO-driven): plants sugarcane at 75/90 cm spacing, performing all major planting operations in one pass. Capacity: 1 ha in 4–5 hours; saves approximately **60% of planting operation cost**.
2. **Three-row multipurpose sugarcane cutter-planter** (ground-wheel driven): plants at 75 cm spacing. Capacity: 1 ha in 3.5–4 hours; saves approximately **70% of planting operation cost**.
3. **Paired-row sugarcane cutter-planter** (PTO-driven): plants under paired-row geometry (30:120/150 cm spacing), with adjustable subsequent row spacing. Capacity: 1 ha in 4–5 hours; saves approximately **60% of planting cost**.
4. **Zero-till sugarcane cutter-planter** (PTO-driven): plants at 75/90 cm spacing without requiring separate seedbed preparation, saving both planting operation cost (~60%) and the cost of land preparation.
5. **Two-row pit digger**: makes 25–30 cm deep, 75 cm diameter circular pits at 30 cm spacing for ring-pit planting. Capacity: 150 pits/hour (0.017 ha/hour); saves about 400 man-days/ha and roughly **70% of digging cost** compared to manual digging.
6. **Raised bed seeder**: makes three raised beds for simultaneous wheat sowing (17 cm spacing) plus three furrows (75 cm spacing) for sugarcane planting as needed. Capacity: 0.35–0.40 ha/hour.
7. **Raised bed seeder-cum-sugarcane cutter planter**: plants two rows of sugarcane in furrows plus intercropped wheat rows on the raised beds in a single pass. Capacity: 0.20–0.25 ha/hour; saves approximately 60% of operation cost.
8. **Ratoon Management Device (RMD)**: executes stubble shaving, deep tilling, off-barring, manure/fertilizer/bio-agent/chemical placement, and earthing-up in a single pass. Capacity: 0.35–0.40 ha/hour; saves about 60% of ratoon-management operation cost.
9. **Plant residue shredder**: performs in-situ shredding of dry sugarcane leaves in the field, with simultaneous stubble shaving capability.
10. **Sugarcane detrasher**: power-operated machine for removing green top and dry trash from harvested cane stalks. Output: 2.4 tonnes/hour, with trash removal efficiency of 77.5–94.5%.
11. **Aerated Steam / Moist Hot Air Treatment (MHAT) equipment**: used to protect setts from sett-borne diseases via hot-air treatment at 54°C with high humidity for 4 hours (an alternative to standard hot-water treatment at 50°C for 2 hours).
12. **Sprayers and dusters**: wide-swath spray booms for tall crop, self-propelled boom sprayers, bucket-type sprayers, knapsack sprayers; bellows-type dusters, shoulder-carried hand-operated rotary dusters, sigma dusters, and rotary hand/power dusters.

## Important Recommendations
- Farmers planting larger areas (multiple hectares) should prioritize the ridger-type or three-row cutter-planter over manual planting, given the documented 60–70% cost saving and multi-fold time reduction (a full hectare planted in under 5 hours versus days of manual labour).
- The Ratoon Management Device is particularly high-value for farmers running multi-ratoon cycles, since it consolidates five distinct operations (stubble shaving, tilling, off-barring, input placement, earthing-up) into a single pass, directly addressing the labour-availability constraint noted in IISR's own ratoon management guidance (ratoon management season often coincides with peak wheat-sowing labour demand in subtropical regions).
- Where sett-borne disease risk is high (see Disease Management document), the Moist Hot Air Treatment (MHAT) equipment offers a mechanized alternative to manual hot-water treatment, potentially more consistent for larger seed-multiplication operations.

## Key Facts
- Most IISR-developed cutter-planters deliver 60–70% cost savings versus manual/conventional planting.
- Pit digger for ring-pit planting saves ~400 man-days/ha versus manual digging.
- Sugarcane detrasher throughput: 2.4 tonnes/hour, 77.5–94.5% trash removal efficiency.
