# Sugarcane Seed Selection, Seed Rate, and Seed Treatment

## Summary
Sugarcane is vegetatively propagated using stem cuttings ("setts"), making seed quality, seed rate, and pre-planting treatment critical determinants of germination and final yield. IISR's package of practices documents optimum seed rates and spacing by state, along with seed treatment protocols to protect against soil-borne pests and diseases and to improve germination percentage.

## Main Information
- Even under satisfactory conditions, only about 40–45% of buds on planted setts typically germinate, making seed health and treatment central to improving stand establishment.
- **Seed rate and spacing vary by region.** For Karnataka (tropical/peninsular zone): recommended seed rate is 25,000–30,000 three-bud setts per hectare, with row spacing of about 90 cm. For comparison, Tamil Nadu uses 42,000–45,000 setts/ha at 80 cm spacing, and subtropical Uttar Pradesh uses 35,000 setts/ha (timely planting, 90 cm spacing) or up to 56,000 setts/ha for late planting at 60 cm spacing.
- **Seed treatment recommendations:**
  1. Always use disease-free, quality setts for planting.
  2. To protect against termites and ants, treat seed cane/stalk with chlorpyriphos at 1.0 kg a.i./ha.
  3. To reduce soil-borne disease incidence, setts should first be given heat treatment (50°C for 2 hours) and then treated with fungicides such as a 0.2% solution of Bavistin (carbendazim).
  4. If setts are infested with scale insects or woolly aphids, dip them in Chlorpyriphos 20 EC solution (2 ml/litre) before planting.
  5. Soaking whole seed cane in normal water for 12–18 hours improves germination by 12–20%, particularly under late planting conditions; this is more beneficial than soaking cut setts alone. Sett treatment with a 10% solution of KMnO₄, MgSO₄, or potassium ferrocyanide accelerates bud sprouting.
  6. To boost biological nitrogen fixation and phosphate solubility, setts should be treated with nitrogen-supplying biofertilizers or phosphate-solubilizing inoculants — for one hectare, dissolve 10 kg of microbial inoculant (CFU >10⁷–10⁸ per ml) in 200–250 litres of water and dip setts for 10–15 minutes before planting.

## Important Recommendations
- Use only disease-free nursery-derived setts, ideally sourced via advanced multiplication techniques (see Planting Methods document for nursery/bud-chip techniques) rather than random field cane, to minimize sett-borne disease carryover (red rot, smut, wilt, grassy shoot disease, ratoon stunting disease).
- Match seed rate and spacing to the specific agro-climatic zone and planting season — Karnataka farmers should use 25,000–30,000 setts/ha at ~90 cm spacing under normal conditions, adjusting for late planting or drought-prone light soils.
- Combine chemical seed treatment (fungicide + insecticide) with bio-inoculant treatment for both disease/pest protection and improved nutrient-use efficiency.

## Key Facts
- Typical bud germination rate without improved practices: only 40–45%.
- Karnataka recommended seed rate: 25,000–30,000 three-bud setts/ha; row spacing ~90 cm.
- Heat treatment standard: 50°C for 2 hours, prior to fungicide dip.
- Water-soaking of whole cane (12–18 hours) can raise germination by 12–20%.
