# Land Preparation for Sugarcane Cultivation

## Summary
Because sugarcane occupies the field for over a year, thorough land preparation — deep ploughing, repeated harrowing, and periodic cross sub-soiling — is essential to break hard pans, improve water infiltration, and support deep root penetration. IISR recommends specific ploughing and sub-soiling schedules tailored to sugarcane's long crop duration.

## Main Information
- As sugarcane stands in the field for more than a year, deep ploughing with a tractor-drawn mould board plough is necessary. The proper time for ploughing is immediately after the preceding crop is harvested, or just after a good shower of rain.
- After ploughing, land should be exposed to the atmosphere for about a month before further operations.
- Harrowing should be done 3 to 4 times to break clods and produce a smooth, even seedbed that facilitates uniform irrigation.
- A total of four to six ploughings is recommended to achieve good tilth, with each ploughing followed by planking (levelling).
- **Cross Sub-Soiling**: An advanced land-preparation method in which cross sub-soiling at 1.0 m spacing is done once every three to four years, before regular field preparation, using a tractor-drawn sub-soiler to a depth of 45–50 cm. This is followed by planking to break clods before preparing the seedbed.

## Important Recommendations
1. Time ploughing to immediately follow harvest of the preceding crop or a good rain shower, then let the field rest for about a month before further tillage.
2. Carry out 4–6 ploughings with planking after each pass to achieve fine tilth suitable for uniform irrigation.
3. Adopt cross sub-soiling (1.0 m spacing, 45–50 cm depth) once every 3–4 years to break hard pans that restrict root penetration and water infiltration — this measure is specifically recommended for enhancing both plant-cane and ratoon-cane yields.

## Key Facts
- Recommended sub-soiling depth: 45–50 cm.
- Recommended sub-soiling interval: once every 3–4 years.
- Recommended number of ploughings for good tilth: 4–6, each followed by planking.
