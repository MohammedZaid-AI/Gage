# Weed Management in Sugarcane (Plant and Ratoon Crops)

## Summary
Weed competition for nutrients, moisture, and space is a major cause of sugarcane yield loss. IISR's package of practices recommends an integrated approach combining pre-emergence herbicides, post-emergence herbicides, and manual hoeing, with specific schedules for plant crop, ratoon crop, and control of binding weeds.

## Main Information
- **Weed management in the plant crop**: Metribuzin (1.0 kg a.i./ha) or ametryn (2.0 kg a.i./ha) applied as pre-emergence herbicide is as effective as the earlier-recommended atrazine (2.0 kg a.i./ha). Either should be combined with 2,4-D (1.0 kg a.i./ha) applied at 60 days after planting (DAP), plus one hoeing at 90 DAP — this combination sustains cane yield equivalent to three manual hoeings at 30, 60, and 90 DAP.
- **Weed management in ratoon crop**: Three hoeings at 1, 4, and 7 weeks after ratoon initiation are recommended for effective weed control. Where labour/cost is limiting, pre-emergence atrazine (2.0 kg a.i./ha) or metribuzin (1.0 kg a.i./ha) in 800–1000 litres water/ha, followed by 2,4-D sodium salt (1.0 kg a.i./ha) or hoeing at 45 days after ratooning, is an effective alternative. Trash mulching in alternate rows combined with hoeing in the unmulched furrow at 1 and 6 weeks after ratoon initiation is also effective.
- **Management of binding weeds**: Atrazine (2 kg a.i./ha) or metribuzin (1.25 kg a.i./ha) as pre-emergence, followed by dicamba (350 g a.i./ha) at 75 DAP, is effective against binding-type weeds.
- **Hoeing and earthing-up**: Blind hoeing about one week after planting breaks the surface crust to aid seedling emergence. Subsequent hoeings occur roughly every 6–8 weeks in coordination with fertilizer top-dressing. Earthing-up suppresses excess tillers, aids irrigation efficiency, drains excess water, controls weeds, and prevents lodging.

## Important Recommendations
- Adopt an integrated approach — pre-emergence herbicide + one post-emergence herbicide application + a single supplementary hoeing — rather than relying solely on repeated manual weeding, to achieve equivalent weed control at lower labour cost.
- For ratoon crops specifically, combine trash mulching with hoeing at defined intervals (1 and 6 weeks after ratoon initiation) rather than chemical control alone, since mulching also conserves moisture and suppresses weeds simultaneously.
- Time the first (blind) hoeing to about one week after planting to break the surface crust and support uniform germination.

## Key Facts
- Recommended pre-emergence herbicide options: metribuzin (1.0 kg a.i./ha) or ametryn (2.0 kg a.i./ha), equally effective as atrazine (2.0 kg a.i./ha).
- Post-emergence standard: 2,4-D at 1.0 kg a.i./ha, applied at 60 DAP (plant crop).
- Ratoon crop: three hoeings at 1, 4, and 7 weeks after ratoon initiation is the manual-control benchmark.
