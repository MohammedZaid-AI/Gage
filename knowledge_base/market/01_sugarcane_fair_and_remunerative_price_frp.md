# Sugarcane Market Price: Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) System

## Summary
Unlike most agricultural commodities that rely purely on open-market price discovery, sugarcane in India is protected by a statutory minimum price mechanism — the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) — which sugar mills are legally required to pay farmers. The FRP is announced annually by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) before each crushing season, based on Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) recommendations.

## Main Information
- **What FRP is**: the minimum price that sugar mills must legally pay to sugarcane farmers ("Gannakisan") for cane supplied for crushing, for a given basic sugar recovery rate. It replaced the earlier Statutory Minimum Price (SMP) concept following an amendment to the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966, on 22 October 2009.
- **Legal basis**: FRP operates under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955, and the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966, ensuring price parity and farmer protection irrespective of whether individual sugar mills are profitable.
- **How it is set**: recommended by the CACP (an attached office of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, established January 1965), in consultation with state governments and sugar industry associations, and finally approved by the CCEA.
- **Recovery-linked premium/penalty structure**: FRP is fixed for a "basic recovery rate" (currently 10.25%), with a premium added for every 0.1% of recovery above this rate, and a corresponding reduction for every 0.1% below it. However, the government has built in a floor: **no deduction is applied where a mill's recovery falls below 9.5%** — farmers supplying such mills still receive a guaranteed minimum FRP.
- **Historical FRP trend (₹/quintal, at 10.25% recovery)**: ₹210 (2013-14) → ₹220 (2014-15) → ₹230 (2015-16, 2016-17) → ₹255 (2017-18) → ₹275 (2018-19, 2019-20) → ₹285 (2020-21) → ₹290 (2021-22) → ₹305 (2022-23) → ₹315 (2023-24) → ₹340 (2024-25) → ₹355 (2025-26) → ₹365 (2026-27, approved).
- **Cost-of-production comparison**: for 2025-26, the A2+FL cost of production was ₹173/quintal against an FRP of ₹355/quintal — meaning FRP was set 105.2% above production cost. For 2026-27, production cost is ₹182/quintal against an FRP of ₹365/quintal (100.5% above cost).
- **State Advised Price (SAP)**: some states fix a SAP higher than the central FRP; where this applies, mills must pay the higher of the two figures.
- **Payment discipline**: sugar mills are legally required to pay farmers within 14 days of cane delivery; delayed payment attracts interest liability and can risk licence cancellation for the mill.
- **Sector scale**: the sugarcane/sugar sector affects the livelihoods of about 5 crore sugarcane farmers and their dependents, plus roughly 5 lakh workers directly employed in sugar mills, apart from farm labour and transportation workers.
- **Payment track record**: in sugar season 2023-24, of ₹1,11,782 crore in cane dues payable, about ₹1,11,703 crore (99.92%) had been paid to farmers as of the most recent reporting cycle cited.

## Important Recommendations
- Sugarcane farmers should track the **announced FRP for the current sugar season** (October–September cycle) rather than relying on the previous year's figure, since FRP is revised annually and the applicable rate is announced before each 1 October start of the crushing season.
- Because payment is legally due within 14 days of delivery, farmers experiencing payment delays beyond this window have a formal basis to escalate to the sugar mill/state cane commissioner, since delayed payment triggers a statutory interest liability on the mill.
- Farmers should check whether their state has fixed a State Advised Price (SAP) higher than the central FRP, since where a SAP exists and exceeds FRP, mills are obligated to pay the higher SAP rate.

## Key Facts
- FRP for sugar season 2025-26: ₹355/quintal at 10.25% basic recovery rate; floor price at recovery below 9.5%: ₹329.05/quintal.
- FRP for sugar season 2026-27: ₹365/quintal at 10.25% basic recovery rate; floor price at recovery below 9.5%: ₹338.3/quintal.
- Premium/penalty per 0.1% recovery change (2025-26): ₹3.46/quintal; (2026-27): ₹3.56/quintal.
- Mandatory payment window: within 14 days of cane delivery.
