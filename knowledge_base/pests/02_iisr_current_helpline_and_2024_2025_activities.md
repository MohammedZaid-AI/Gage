# ICAR-IISR Current Farmer Helpline and 2024–2025 Institutional Activities

## Summary
ICAR-Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research (IISR), Lucknow operates an active, current farmer helpline for real-time sugarcane production and protection queries, and has run a series of dated 2024–2025 training programs and awareness campaigns specifically on sugarcane pest management, mechanization, and seed material supply — providing a current-activity supplement to the institute's 2017 technical bulletin already in this knowledge base.

## Main Information
- **Farmer Helpline (current, ongoing service)**: farmers may send text messages (SMS) as well as visuals (photographs) for problems related to crop production and insect-pest/disease occurrence, using a mobile phone or email, addressed to the Nodal Officer for farmer advisory services at ICAR-IISR, Raibareli Road, Lucknow.
- **Sugarcane seed sales (current cycle)**: online applications for sale of sugarcane seed (spring planting) were accepted starting 1 February 2025, confirming an active, dated seed-supply cycle for the current season.
- **Dated 2024–2025 training and awareness activities** (confirming current institutional focus areas, useful as evidence of which pest/disease/technology topics IISR is actively prioritizing right now):
  - One-day Farmer Training Programme on "Identification and Management of Major Sugarcane Borers" under SC-SP Plan, at ICAR-IISR Biological Control Centre, Pravaranagar (Maharashtra).
  - One-day Farmer Training Programme on "Integrated Management of White Grubs Infesting Sugarcane," at the same Biological Control Centre.
  - Training program on "Biological Control of Pests in Sugarcane," organized by the ICAR-IISR Biological Control Centre, Pravaranagar.
  - Launch of "Parthenium Awareness Week" (16–22 August 2024) by ICAR-IISR — a weed-management/invasive-species public awareness initiative.
  - National Seminar on "Mechanization of Sugarcane Cultivation," and a five-day residential training programme titled "Importance of Mechanization in Sugarcane-Based Cropping Systems" (Hindi: गन्ना आधारित फसल प्रणाली में यांत्रिकीकरण का महत्व) — both reinforcing mechanization as a current institutional priority, consistent with the machinery documents already in this knowledge base.
  - ICAR-IISR, Lucknow was conferred the Agriculture Leadership Award 2024.
- These activities collectively confirm that, as of the 2024–2025 period, biological pest control (borers, white grubs), weed awareness (Parthenium), and mechanization remain IISR's active extension priorities — directly reinforcing (rather than contradicting) the pest management and machinery guidance already sourced from the institute's 2017 bulletin.

## Important Recommendations
- Farmers or advisory-system builders seeking real-time, query-specific sugarcane troubleshooting (as opposed to general package-of-practices guidance) should be directed to IISR's SMS/photo-based farmer helpline rather than relying solely on static documents, since this channel provides current, personalized diagnosis.
- Given IISR's active 2024 focus on biological control training for borers and white grubs, any advisory content on these two pests (see the Pest Management document) should be treated as current best practice, not superseded guidance — the institute's own 2024 programming reinforces rather than revises the 2017 bulletin's biological-control recommendations for these specific pests.
- The confirmed active 2025 seed-sale cycle (applications open from February 2025) indicates that IISR remains a viable current channel for procuring certified planting material, relevant to the Seed Selection document's recommendation to source disease-free nursery-derived setts.

## Key Facts
- Sugarcane seed (spring planting) online applications opened 1 February 2025 — confirms an active, current-season seed distribution channel.
- 2024 dated activities: Parthenium Awareness Week (16–22 August 2024), sugarcane borer identification training, white grub management training, Agriculture Leadership Award 2024.
- Farmer helpline channel: SMS/photo submission via mobile phone or email to the IISR Nodal Officer.
