# Integrated Insect-Pest Management in Sugarcane

## Summary
Sugarcane is attacked by a range of insect pests including pyrilla, woolly aphid, stalk/internode borer, top borer, white grub, early shoot/root borer, termite, whitefly, mealy bug, and rodents. IISR's package of practices documents a five-pronged integrated management approach (chemical, biological, bio-technical, agronomic, and physical/quarantine measures) with specific bio-agent release rates and chemical dosages for each major pest.

## Main Information
Integrated pest management approaches recommended:
1. **Chemical measures**: fungicides, nematicides, insecticides, herbicides, bio-regulators.
2. **Biological measures**: beneficial arthropods, pathogens, antagonists, resistant cultivars, organic fertilizers.
3. **Bio-technical measures**: pheromones, insect hormones, sterile male techniques.
4. **Agronomic practices**: crop rotation, elimination of inoculum sources/alternate hosts, proper planting.
5. **Physical procedures**: mechanical, thermal, and exclusion (netting) methods, plus plant quarantine (import/export control).

### Pest-specific management
- **Pyrilla**: Release of the biocontrol agent *Epiricania melanoleuca* at 4,000–5,000 cocoons/ha is effective.
- **Woolly Aphid**: Effectively managed by releasing bio-agents such as *Dipha aphidivora* (1,000 larvae/ha) or *Micromus igorotus* (2,000 larvae/ha) at 15-day intervals from August to October. Need-based chemical options: imidacloprid 200SL (100 g a.i./ha), chlorpyriphos 20 EC (1 kg a.i./ha), oxydemeton methyl 25 EC (1 kg a.i./ha), or thiamethoxam 25 WG (50 g a.i./ha).
- **Sugarcane stalk and internode borer**: Release *Trichogramma chilonis* at 50,000 adults/ha (two Trichocards) at 10-day intervals from July to October, and *Cotesia flavipes* at 500 gravid females/ha/week from July to November.
- **Top borer**: Soil application of Thimet 10G at 30 kg/ha (1 kg a.i./ha) around the clump base during the 2nd–3rd week of June, combined with pheromone trapping of adult moths.
- **White grub**: Light traps plus pheromone traps to catch adult beetles.
- **Early shoot and root borer**: Application of Chlorpyriphos 20 EC at 5 litres/ha in 1,600–1,800 litres water (3 ml/litre) on cane setts in furrows at planting. Collection and destruction of infested shoots on a campaign basis from March to May is also recommended.
- **Termite**: Chlorpyriphos 20 EC at 5 litres/ha in 1,600–1,800 litres water applied over setts in furrows at planting.
- **Whitefly**: Removal of lower leaves followed by foliar application of imidacloprid (0.005%) + 2% urea; urea solution should be prepared first, then imidacloprid added, to avoid coagulation.
- **Mealy bug**: Sett treatment with imidacloprid 70 WG/SP or thiamethoxam 70 WG/SP (25 g a.i./ha), followed by foliar spraying of imidacloprid 17.8 SL (0.05%) at cane formation stage.
- **Rodents**: Zinc phosphide (2%) baiting followed by bromadiolone baiting.

## Important Recommendations
- Farmers should adopt an integrated combination of biological release agents (Trichogramma, Cotesia, Epiricania) and targeted chemical spot-treatments rather than relying on blanket insecticide spraying, since the source data shows biological control is the primary recommendation for pyrilla, woolly aphid, and stalk borer.
- Time interventions to pest biology: e.g., top borer soil treatment specifically in the 2nd–3rd week of June, and biocontrol releases for stalk borer from July through October/November — aligning treatment windows with pest life cycles rather than applying controls reactively after damage is visible.
- Always treat setts with chlorpyriphos at planting as a preventive measure against termite and early shoot/root borer, since both are soil-borne pests best controlled prophylactically rather than after infestation.

## Key Facts
- Pyrilla biocontrol release rate: 4,000–5,000 cocoons of *Epiricania melanoleuca* per hectare.
- Stalk borer biocontrol: *Trichogramma chilonis* at 50,000 adults/ha at 10-day intervals (July–October).
- Standard sett-furrow insecticide treatment (termite/early shoot borer): Chlorpyriphos 20 EC, 5 litres/ha in 1,600–1,800 litres water.
