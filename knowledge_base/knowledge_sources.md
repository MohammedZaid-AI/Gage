# Knowledge Sources Summary

## Overview
This knowledge base was assembled for the research project *"A Code-Mixed Kannada-English Large Language Model for Intelligent Agricultural Advisory in the Sugarcane Ecosystem."* Following an explicit recency mandate, this version prioritizes documents published or updated in **2024–2026**, and retains pre-2024 material only where no current official replacement could be located for that specific topic. Every retained pre-2024 document is flagged accordingly in `knowledge_base_index.csv` via the **Latest Available? (Yes/No)** column, and the reason for retention is stated below.

## Document Count
- **Total documents collected: 25 markdown files**, spanning 15 topic folders (3 new documents added in this revision).
- **Documents dated 2024–2026 (Latest Available = Yes): 7**
- **Documents pre-2024 or undated, retained only as fallback (Latest Available = No): 18**

## What Changed in This Revision
1. **Added three genuinely current (2024–2025) documents:**
   - `sugarcane/06_icar_kharif_agro_advisory_2025_sugarcane.md` — from *ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025* (published May 2025), ICAR's current national season-advisory publication compiled with State Agricultural Universities and KVKs.
   - `government_schemes/02_viksit_krishi_sankalp_abhiyan_vksa_2025.md` — documenting the active VKSA-2025 KVK/farmer-scientist outreach campaign (May–June 2025), including its confirmed Karnataka launch.
   - `pests/02_iisr_current_helpline_and_2024_2025_activities.md` — documenting ICAR-IISR's live farmer helpline and dated 2024–2025 training/awareness activity (sugarcane borer and white grub training, Parthenium Awareness Week, mechanization seminar, active Feb 2025 seed-sale cycle).

Similarly, the PMFBY premium/coverage detail previously duplicated in the Government Schemes document now cross-references the dedicated Insurance document.
3. **Reliability re-assessment**: two Karnataka Department of Agriculture documents (Government Schemes and Farm Machinery subsidy) were downgraded from High to **Medium** reliability specifically because their exact publication/update date could not be confirmed — under the current recency-first mandate, undated government content is treated more cautiously than explicitly dated content, even when the issuing authority is fully trustworthy.
4. **Added the `Latest Available? (Yes/No)` column** to `knowledge_base_index.csv` for every row, per the current instruction.

## Why 18 Documents Remain Pre-2024 (Latest Available = No)
Per the instruction to include older documents "only when no newer official document exists for that topic," each retained pre-2024 document was kept because:
- **12 documents derive from ICAR-IISR's 2017 "Sugarcane in India: Package of Practices" bulletin** (land preparation, seed selection, planting methods, weed management, irrigation, fertilizer, nutrient deficiency, pests [base], diseases, harvesting, soil, machinery). A genuinely current national alternative was located (*ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025*) and has been added as a **supplementary current document** for sugarcane cultivation specifically, but it does not fully replace the 2017 bulletin's Karnataka-zone-specific technical thresholds (exact NPK doses, pest dosages, disease protocols), which remain the best available Karnataka-relevant figures found in this pass. **These should be treated as provisional until a current (2024–2026) Karnataka- or AICRP-Mandya-specific package of practices is located** (see Gaps section).
- **1 document** (SBI Coimbatore post-harvest management) has no confirmed publication date and no newer official replacement was found for sugarcane-specific post-harvest sucrose-loss management.
- **2 Karnataka Department of Agriculture documents** (general schemes; machinery subsidy) are undated on their source pages; a current-dated replacement (e.g., a specific 2025-26 Government Order) was not located in this pass, so the existing content is retained but downgraded to Medium reliability.
- **1 ILO Code of Practice** on agricultural labour safety has no confirmed recent revision date, and no current (2024–2026) Indian government-issued equivalent was located in this pass.
- **1 women's agricultural health document** (PMC-indexed) has an unconfirmed original publication date; no current (2024–2026) replacement specific to women's health in Indian sugarcane/agricultural labour was located.
- **1 IISR mechanization document** (2017) remains the primary technical source for machine specifications and cost-saving percentages; the new 2024-2025 IISR activity document confirms mechanization remains an active institutional priority but does not itself restate the technical specifications.

## Data Handling Notes
- All documents preserve original agricultural recommendations, figures, and technical thresholds exactly as stated in source material; no scientific facts, dosages, or figures were rewritten or approximated.
- Duplicate recommendations across documents have been consolidated with cross-references rather than repeated verbatim (see "What Changed" above).
- Every document includes a direct source URL, organization attribution, and — as of this revision — an explicit Latest Available status to support recency-aware retrieval in a downstream RAG pipeline (e.g., a system could be configured to prefer Latest Available = Yes chunks when both an old and new source address the same query).
