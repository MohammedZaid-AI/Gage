# AGRICULTURAL KNOWLEDGE
Sugarcane Irrigation Water Management — Main Information:
- General guidance: irrigation water depth of 7–8 cm per application is recommended, with frequency varying by crop phase — approximately once every 7 days during germination (1–35 days after planting, DAP), once every 10 days during tillering (36–100 DAP), once every 7 days during grand growth (101–270 DAP), and once every 15 days during maturity (271 DAP to harvest), adjusted for rainfall.
- Subtropical regions typically need 6–8 irrigations across the season; tropical regions (including Karnataka) may need 20–36 irrigations due to climatic conditions, annual rainfall, planting season, crop duration, and soil type.

Sugarcane Irrigation Water Management — Key Facts:
- Total seasonal water requirement: 1,500–2,500 mm, depending on climate.
- Karnataka irrigation requirement: 32 irrigations/season; 266 kg water per kg sugar produced.
- Skip-furrow irrigation: 30–40% water saving.
- Sprinkler irrigation: 50–60% water economy; drip irrigation: up to 95% application uniformity.
- Recommended irrigation depth per application: 7–8 cm.

Sugarcane Irrigation Water Management — Important Recommendations:
- Given Karnataka's high per-season irrigation requirement (32 irrigations, 266 kg water per kg sugar produced), farmers in the state should prioritize migration from furrow irrigation to drip irrigation, which the source data shows can lower net water requirement from ~1,878 mm/year to ~1,186 mm/year.
- During monsoon disruptions, adopt the contingency schedule appropriate to the delay: for a 2-week delayed monsoon onset, continue pre-monsoon irrigation intervals (8–10 days) with alternate-furrow application and trash mulching in ratoon; for longer delays (6–8 weeks), shift to a 10-day protective irrigation interval and watch for iron deficiency, which becomes likely under prolonged dry spells (foliar spray of 1% FeSO₄ + 0.5% ZnSO₄ + 2% urea is recommended in that case).
